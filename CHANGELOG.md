# iSAQB® Advanced Level Curriculum COSMO

## 2026.1 — Initial Release

This inaugural public release introduces the COSMO Advanced Level module for the CPSA® program. The curriculum equips architects and development teams with the knowledge and practical skills needed to use collaborative modelling effectively in everyday architecture work, combining architectural thinking with facilitation, communication, and collaborative decision-making to create shared understanding and improve the quality of architectural outcomes.

**Language availability**: available at launch in both German and English.

### Curriculum Structure

The curriculum comprises six progressive learning units, plus a chapter of worked examples:

1. **Software Development as a Social System** — introduces the social and collaborative nature of software architecture work
2. **Fundamentals of Collaborative Modeling** — core terms, principles, and the basics of running collaborative modelling sessions
3. **Advanced Collaborative Modelling** — deepening knowledge through advanced concepts and practices
4. **Succeeding with Collaborative Modeling** — creating the conditions for the long-term success of collaborative sessions
5. **Creating Impact as Facilitator** — developing the facilitation skills needed to achieve lasting impact
6. **Overview of Collaborative Techniques** — a survey of collaborative modelling techniques and practices
7. **Examples** — worked examples illustrating collaborative modelling in practice
